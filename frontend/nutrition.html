Indian Culinary Superfoods & Functional Biochemistry

Nutrient-dense whole foods deeply anchored in traditional regional cooking.

Whole Foods
A

Amla (Gooseberry)

Vitamin C & Antioxidants

Contains 20x the vitamin C of an orange. Enhances intestinal absorption of non-heme iron from palak and lentils.

Pairs: Palak, Moong
T

Turmeric (Haldi)

Curcumin Polyphenols

Supports hepatic antioxidant defenses and nuclear factor-kappa B modulation. Always pair with black pepper (piperine) for 2000% higher bioavailability.

Pairs: Pepper, Dal
R

Ragi (Finger Millet)

Calcium & Low GI Carb

Exceptional calcium content (344 mg/100g) and slow-digesting resistant starch for steady postprandial glucose regulation.

Pairs: Buttermilk, Dosa
M

Sprouted Moong

Bioavailable Protein

Sprouting deactivates phytates, boosting bioavailability of zinc, iron, and folate. Highly recommended for Jain and vegan profiles.

Pairs: Cumin, Lemon
D

Moringa (Sahjan)

Micro-Nutrient Density

Abundant in plant-based iron, vitamin A, potassium, and quercetin. Traditional addition to sambar and lentil preparations.

Pairs: Sambar, Rasam
