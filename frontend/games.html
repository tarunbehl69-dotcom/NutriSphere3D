Laboratory Biomarker:

ALT (SGPT)

Which organ releases this enzyme into your blood when its cells are stressed or processing medications?

💡 Clinical Plain-English Explanation

ALT (Alanine Aminotransferase) lives inside liver cells. When liver cells experience temporary stress, ALT enters the bloodstream.
